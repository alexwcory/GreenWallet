# GreenWallet: A Blockchain Efficiency Tool

## Introduction
Blockchain technology, at its core, is built on the principles of trustlessness and immutability. These principles have enabled a decentralized and secure digital ecosystem where on-chain transactions and data are transparent, tamper-proof, and verifiable without relying on centralized entities. However, as blockchain adoption has grown, so have concerns about energy consumption, transaction efficiency, and the environmental impact of blockchain activity.  

GreenWallet seeks to address these concerns by providing users with a composite score that evaluates their efficiency in using blockchain technology. By publicly promoting energy-conscious and efficient practices, GreenWallet empowers users to adopt sustainable behaviors while leveraging the decentralized power of blockchain.  

## Project Overview
GreenWallet is an Ethereum-based tool that monitors wallet activity and assigns an efficiency score based on blockchain usage patterns. The tool evaluates key metrics such as energy consumption, transaction volume, and multi-chain behavior to calculate a composite score representing a user's efficiency.

GreenWallet leverages core blockchain tenets to ensure that all evaluations and metrics are trustless, transparent, and immutable. It assigns Soulbound Tokens (SBTs) as verifiable indicators of a user's blockchain efficiency, enabling users to showcase their sustainability credentials to their network of peers in the Web3 ecosystem.

## Key Features
### 1. Composite Blockchain Efficiency Score (Exact Algorithm TBD)
  GreenWallet calculates a composite score based on:
  - Energy Consumption: Measures the watt-hour (Wh) usage of transactions across various chains.
  - Transaction Patterns: Tracks transaction volume, frequency, and type.
  - Multi-Chain Behavior: Observes activity across multiple chains like Bitcoin, Solana, and Ethereum to gauge overall blockchain engagement.

### 2. Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)
  Users receive non-transferable Soulbound Tokens that act as digital badges for their blockchain efficiency. 
  These SBTs are tied to the user's wallet and serve as immutable proof of their eco-friendly blockchain usage.

### 3. Cross-Chain Monitoring (In Progress)
  GreenWallet uses APIs (e.g., MetaMask API and Infura) to track wallet activity across multiple chains. This comprehensive monitoring ensures that efficiency scores reflect a user's full blockchain activity.

### 4. How it Works 
   - Connect a MetaMask wallet to GreenWallet on the web application.
   - GreenWallet retrieves previous transactions and monitors for future transations.
   - GreenWallet calculates an efficiency score based on the retreival and monitoring.
   - If a new tier of efficiency is met, GreenWallet mints an SBT to the wallet.

### 5. Technologies Used
   - Ethereum Blockchain: The foundation for GreenWallet's operations and SBT deployment.
   - Solidity: Smart contracts to implement the scoring system and manage SBT issuance.
   - MetaMask API: Wallet integration and transaction monitoring. (In progress)
   - Infura: Infrastructure for interacting with blockchain nodes. (In progress)
   - Ganache: A local Ethereum blockchain emulator for testing and development.
   - Truffle: A development framework for building, testing, and deploying smart contracts.

### 5. Vision and Goals
   - Harness blockchain technology to produce a trustless and transparent insight into blockchain efficiency.
   - Encourage energy-efficient blockchain use by providing users with actionable feedback.
   - Make blockchain efficiency a standard metric available for peers to view in Web3 networks.
   - Foster a sustainable Web3 ecosystem by promoting eco-conscious behavior.<br>
   
By combining blockchain's foundational principles with a focus on sustainability, GreenWallet aspires to make environmental responsibility a key feature of a decentralized community.
